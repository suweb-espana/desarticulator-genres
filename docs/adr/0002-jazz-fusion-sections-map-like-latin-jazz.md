# Jazz Fusion complete songs map two Joe's Garage grooves into one suite

A progressive Jazz Fusion song is one Joe's Garage suite, not a second genre.
Verse is the Central Scrutinizer Vamp. Chorus is the Catholic Girls Groove.
Bridge is the Scrutinizer B-section: kick mutations, no tom ostinato.
Outro returns to the vamp and ends with fills. The odd-meter Catholic Girls
interlude stays out of the 4/4 grid.

**Considered Options**:
- Register Catholic Girls as its own genre with a second 150-bar complete
- Keep every section as a louder Central Scrutinizer vamp
- Use dense-tom variation 2 as the bridge
- Encode the 9/16–22/16 Catholic Girls interlude as real meters

**Consequences**:
`--section chorus` and the chorus files inside a progressive complete are Catholic Girls.
`--section verse` stays Central Scrutinizer. `--section bridge` has no tom melody.
