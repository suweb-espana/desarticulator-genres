# Desarticulator

Modular MIDI drum-pattern generator for Superior Drummer 3. This glossary names musical identities, not code.

## Language

**Jazz Fusion**:
Vinnie Colaiuta on *Joe's Garage* (1979), two 4/4 grooves: Central Scrutinizer Vamp as the default, Catholic Girls Groove as the other variation. Both mapped for Le Cars.
_Avoid_: Mahavishnu, Billy Cobham, jazz blues, latin jazz, King Crimson as the primary voice, open hat on 3, the odd-meter Catholic Girls bridge as the default 4/4 grid

**Le Cars**:
The Rock Foundry SDX preset this MIDI is written for: Ludwig Vistalite kick, Brady snare, 18" crash hats, 24" bash ride, four Ayotte toms. Only loaded GM Extended notes sound.
_Avoid_: china on note 52, splash on note 55, 14" tight hats, pingy jazz ride, open hat as timekeeper color

**Central Scrutinizer Vamp**:
The default Jazz Fusion groove: eighth-note hats, backbeat, tom melody on the and of 1 and on 3, Closed Accent on 3, sixteenth kick variations especially on beat 3.
_Avoid_: ride as the clock, toms only in fills, sixteenth-note hats as the default, MIDI note 46 on beat 3

**Catholic Girls Groove**:
The other Jazz Fusion variation, and the chorus of a complete song: 4/4 intro and chorus of *Catholic Girls* — driving eighth hats, rock kick, snare on 2 and 4, ghosts, no tom ostinato.
_Avoid_: 9/16 7/8 11/16 bridge, sixteenth-note hats, tom melody from the Central Scrutinizer Vamp

**Closed Accent**:
A louder closed hi-hat on beat 3 of the Central Scrutinizer Vamp. It replaces Vinnie's open hat so Le Cars 18" crash hats do not wash.
_Avoid_: open hat, MIDI note 46 as the beat-3 color

**Prog Rock**:
Irregular rock drumming already in the project: hats, snare on 2 and 4, 5-against-4 and 7-against-4 groupings. Distinct from Jazz Fusion.
_Avoid_: Jazz Fusion, Joe's Garage
